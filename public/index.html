      Field guide: obstacles & items

      Obstacles

      • 404 pit — fall in, respawn after a moment
      • Broken link — trip and spin out
      • Merge conflict — stop to resolve it
      • Infinite scroll — a conveyor dragging you back
      • Slow load zone — crawl at 99% loaded
      • Redirect loop — go round in circles
      • Null pointer — a sweeping cursor turns you null
      • Unhandled exception — a mine that throws everyone nearby
      • SSL Expired — a "not private" warning page stops you
      • DO NOT MODIFY URLS — a barricade that drops you into an error pit for a few seconds
      • 800-Column Table — juts in from the edge; the first racer near it sets off BULK LOAD IN PROGRESS and it spreads across half the track. Clip it and lose your items. Truncated after 10 seconds

      Items (from {?} crates)

      • ☕ Coffee — speed boost
      • 💨 Bay Breeze — big tailwind for those behind
      • 🛡️ Firewall — blocks the next hit
      • 🐛 Bug — thrown at the racer ahead
      • 💥 Force Push — flattens the leader
      • 🔐 Renewed Certificate — immune to SSL Expired while it lasts
      • ↩️ ?returnUrl= — takes you through one DO NOT MODIFY URLS barricade
      • 🌫️ You Are Not Being Clear — thrown at a rival; they spin out trying to clarify. "It's your fault."
      • 📋 Eight-Minute Audit — locks a rival's controls for 8 seconds. "Access limited pending audit."
      • 🎰 Production Roulette — reverses a rival's steering. "Oops. That was pr0dI."
      • 🎭 Security Theater — locks a rival to 12–15 mph. "Vahi says you're going too fast."
      • 📊 200GB Table — thrown at the racer ahead; it flattens them for a moment. "You're wasting disk space."
